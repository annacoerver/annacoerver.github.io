I contribute to the R&D of SPT-3G+, the next-generation receiver for the South Pole Telescope. SPT-3G+ will field ~24,000 detectors across 90 and 150 GHz to constrain the tensor-to-scalar ratio r to σ(r) = 0.001. At Berkeley, we are focusing on the integration and noise characterization of detector modules with SMuRF (SQUID multiplexed) readout electronics. Image credit: Natoli et al. 2026
