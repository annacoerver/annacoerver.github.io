Ice crystals in the upper atmosphere can create large-scale polarized noise in CMB data, contaminating searches for gravitational wave B-modes. We published the first complete characterization of this effect, including its frequency scaling, spatial scaling, elevation scaling, polarization angle, and amplitude distribution (Coerver et al 2025). This information was used to explicitly mitigate polarized atmospheric noise for the first time in an inflationary B-mode search with SPT-3G, Zebrowski et al. 2025.
