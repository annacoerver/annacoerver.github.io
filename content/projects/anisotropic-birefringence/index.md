I am using data from the South Pole Telescope to search for anisotropic cosmic birefringence, a direction-dependent polarization rotation of the CMB. Cosmic birefringence can be caused by exciting new physics including ultra-light axions and primordial magnetic fields. Anisotropic birefringence measurements offer an exciting and complimentary test of new physics in light of recent hints of isotropic cosmic birefringence in ACT and Planck data. Image credit: Y.
